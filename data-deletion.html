Your Rights

Data Deletion Instructions

How to request removal of personal information the DMPS WhatsApp chatbot has received from or about you.

Under applicable data protection law, you have the right to request deletion of personal information that Deep Memorial Public School holds as a result of your interaction with our WhatsApp chatbot. This includes your name, phone number, any information you shared about your child, and the content of conversations with the chatbot.

To exercise this right, please follow the steps below.

How to Submit a Deletion Request

  1. 1
    Compose your request

    Prepare a brief message or email that includes: your full name, the WhatsApp phone number you used to contact the chatbot, and a clear statement that you are requesting deletion of your data.

  2. 2
    Send it to the school's privacy contact

    Email your request to dmpsdigitalmarketing@gmail.com. You may also send a written request by post or visit the school's administrative office in person.

  3. 3
    Await confirmation

    The school will acknowledge your request within 7 business days. Deletion will be completed within 14 days of receiving a valid request, subject to any legal obligations that require retention.

What Will Be Deleted

Upon a valid deletion request, DMPS will remove or anonymise:

  • Your name and WhatsApp phone number stored in the chatbot system.
  • Conversation records associated with your number.
  • Any enquiry information you provided during the chatbot interaction.

Where records form part of an active admission file or are required by law to be retained, we will inform you of any limitation on deletion and the reason for it.
